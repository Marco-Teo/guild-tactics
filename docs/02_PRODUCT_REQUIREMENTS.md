# Product Requirements

## Public users

Visitors can:

- browse compositions ordered by quality and relevance;
- search by character, item, relic, specialization, effect or creator;
- view composition details, positioning and itemization;
- browse characters, items, relics, patches and tier lists;
- try the builder without permanent saving.

## Registered users

Registered users can:

- save drafts;
- publish compositions;
- edit their own compositions;
- create new patch versions;
- maintain changelogs;
- favorite, vote, comment, follow and report;
- maintain a personal collection;
- receive in-app notifications;
- view basic creator analytics.

## Composition requirements

A publishable composition includes:

- title and patch;
- board and units;
- short description;
- how it works;
- why it works;
- positioning explanation;
- win condition;
- strengths and weaknesses;
- run priorities;
- key characters, items and relics;
- substitutions;
- favorable and unfavorable matchups;
- author notes.

“How it works” and “Why it works” are mandatory.

## Composition classification

- Verified
- Community
- Experimental
- Needs update
- Outdated
- Archived

## Community ranking

Ranking should combine:

- freshness for the current patch;
- guide completeness;
- community feedback;
- saves and views;
- verification status;
- confirmed reports.

Never label internal site usage as official in-game statistics.
