# Project Vision

## Product

Guild Tactics is a mobile-first companion platform for GuildRun.

## Core value

Users can discover, understand, build, publish and maintain team compositions across patches.

## Main audiences

- New players looking for reliable compositions.
- Experienced players researching synergies and positioning.
- Creators publishing and maintaining guides.
- Moderators and editors maintaining data quality.
- Administrators managing patches, assets and structured game data.

## Product pillars

1. Explainability: every composition explains how and why it works.
2. Visual building: TFT Academy-style board with characters and items.
3. Patch awareness: content is versioned and automatically flagged when affected.
4. Community creation: registered users can save, publish and update their own comps.
5. Structured knowledge: characters, items, relics, specializations, buffs and debuffs are searchable entities.
6. Trust: distinguish official, editor-verified, community-verified and unverified information.

## Provisional scope

- Public composition browser.
- Global search.
- Visual composition builder.
- Accounts and creator dashboard.
- Item assignment to units.
- Patch archive and patch impact system.
- Comments, votes, favorites and reports.
- Tier list, collection tracker and internal community meta.
- Administration and moderation.
