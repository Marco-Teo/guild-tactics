# Milestones

## 1 — Foundations

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, component primitives, Docker Compose, PostgreSQL, Prisma, environment configuration, lint, formatter, testing setup, base layout and demo data labeling.

## 2 — Authentication and permissions

Registration, login, logout, sessions, profiles, roles, route protection, ownership and development accounts.

## 3 — Editorial game database

Characters, items, item versions, relics, specializations, patches, structured stats, source metadata, verification state, placeholders and essential admin CRUD.

## 4 — Public compositions

Composition and version models, listing, filters, pagination, search, read-only board, explanations, status badges and history.

## 5 — Base builder

Draft creation, board editing, character picker, desktop and mobile placement, autosave, completeness and preview.

## 6 — Items on units

Three configurable slots, drag/drop, tap flow, tooltip, mobile sheet, alternatives, notes and patch-correct item versions.

## 7 — Publishing and versioning

Validation, publish, changelog, new patch version, archive, duplicate, JSON import/export.

## 8 — Community

Profiles, favorites, voting, comments, follows, reports, moderation and notifications.

## 9 — Patch impact

Structured patch changes, dependency detection, NEEDS_UPDATE, author notification and historical change views.

## 10 — Collection and meta

Personal collection, completion matching, recommendations, internal meta tracker, tier lists and creator analytics.

## 11 — Production hardening

Accessibility, SEO, Open Graph, performance, security, rate limiting, audit logs, upload safety, CI and deployment docs.
